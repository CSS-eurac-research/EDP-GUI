The EDP is a collaborative platform to support researchers and project partners to manage, process and share spatial datasets. It is composed by open-source solutions to provide web services and web tools fostering FAIR data principles. The EDP was developed in the contest of DPS4ESLAB project and is managed by the Center for Sensing Solutions in collaboration with the Institute for Earth Observation.
